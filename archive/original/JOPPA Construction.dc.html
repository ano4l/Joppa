Our Story

A family trade, refined into a residential specialist.

JOPPA Construction began with two decades of hands-on building experience. Working alongside his father, our founder learned the trade the right way — on real sites, solving real problems — before pairing that knowledge with formal tertiary qualifications.

The company was established in 2007, and operates independently as a dedicated residential specialist. Every project carries the same standard: do it properly, finish it beautifully, and leave the homeowner glad they chose us.

From new homes and full renovations to the careful restoration of heritage Victorians over a century old, our work speaks in finishes, not promises.
